How much can we trust the reference?

These are numerical checks, not certified error bounds or a real-world recording. The boundary-element solver is independent of our ray and edge kernels.

What this comparison establishes

The wave solution includes interference and sound bending around finite solids. Without diffraction, the live sampler relies on direct sound and sampled reflections. Higher ray counts test sampling coverage; they do not guarantee the wave model is complete.

Error = ‖native − wave‖₂ / ‖wave‖₂ over the displayed samples; it can exceed 100%. The reference is steady-state. The live sampler has finite depth and time support, so model, sampling and truncation errors are combined.
