The improved solver is still here

The earlier work changed how complete reflection and diffraction routes are sampled and integrated. It substantially improved the wave match in these controlled scenes. Those improvements remained in an offline prototype; the rain demo still uses the older finite-3D sampler shown in the other tabs.

These are archived 21 October measurements at 250 Hz, with a point source and infinitely extruded rigid walls. They are not the finite-3D blocks or canopy, and their error percentages cannot be compared as if the scenes and budgets were identical.
